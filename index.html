Before your consultation

A little preparation helps

Having a few details ready can make the consultation easier and more useful.

What to keep ready

✓ Child's age and current weight, if known
✓ Previous prescriptions or reports
✓ Current medicines, if any
✓ Child's vaccination record

For online consultation

✓ Choose a quiet place where the child can be observed.
✓ Keep relevant reports or photographs available when appropriate.
✓ Describe the main concern briefly in the appointment request.
✓ The clinic will confirm the consultation time.
Vaccination

Keep your child's vaccination history up to date

Vaccination is an important part of preventive healthcare during childhood.

For parents

Keep the child's vaccination record safely and bring it when discussing vaccination history or future vaccination needs.

  • Keep the vaccination record accessible.
  • Discuss missed or delayed doses with your pediatrician.
  • Bring previous vaccination records when possible.

Have a vaccination question?

If you are unsure about your child's vaccination history or upcoming vaccines, request a pediatric consultation and discuss it with the doctor.
